# Service Level Agreement

The Pro plan includes a 99.9% monthly uptime SLA.
The Enterprise plan includes a 99.95% monthly uptime SLA.
The Free and Starter plans do not include an uptime SLA.
If monthly uptime falls below the SLA, customers receive a 10% service credit.
If monthly uptime falls below 99.0%, customers receive a 25% service credit.
Service credits must be requested within 30 days of the incident.
Scheduled maintenance announced 72 hours in advance does not count as downtime.
