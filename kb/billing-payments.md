# Billing and Payment Methods

AcmeCloud accepts Visa, Mastercard and American Express credit cards.
Enterprise customers can pay by invoice with 30-day payment terms.
Invoices are issued on the 1st of each month and can be downloaded from the billing portal.
Accounts can be billed in USD or EUR.
If a payment fails, AcmeCloud retries the charge 3 times over 7 days before suspending the account.
