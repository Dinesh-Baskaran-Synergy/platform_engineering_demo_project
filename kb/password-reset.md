# Resetting Your Password

To reset your password, click "Forgot password" on the login page and enter your account email.
Password reset links are valid for 30 minutes.
If you do not receive the reset email within 10 minutes, check your spam folder.
After 5 failed login attempts the account is locked for 15 minutes.
AcmeCloud support staff will never ask for your password.
If two-factor authentication is enabled, you will need your 2FA code after resetting the password.
