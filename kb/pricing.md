# Plans and Pricing

AcmeCloud offers four plans: Free, Starter, Pro and Enterprise.
The Free plan costs $0 per month and includes 1 vCPU, 2 GB of RAM and 5 GB of storage.
The Starter plan costs $29 per month and includes 50 GB of storage.
The Pro plan costs $99 per month and includes 1 TB of storage.
Enterprise pricing is custom and is quoted by the sales team at sales@acmecloud.example.
Annual billing gives a 20% discount compared to paying monthly.
Prices are listed in USD and exclude local taxes such as VAT.
Compute overage beyond the plan allowance is billed at $0.02 per vCPU-hour.
