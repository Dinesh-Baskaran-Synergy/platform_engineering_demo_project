# Refund Policy

Monthly plans can be refunded within 14 days of the charge.
Annual plans can be refunded within 30 days of the purchase date.
Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 to 10 business days.
Usage-based charges such as compute overage are non-refundable.
To request a refund, open a billing ticket from the billing portal or ask the support assistant to create one.
Refunds are not available for accounts suspended for abuse.
