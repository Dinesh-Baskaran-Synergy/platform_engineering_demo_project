# Data Retention

Application logs are retained for 30 days.
Automatic backups are kept for 7 days on the Starter plan and 35 days on the Pro plan.
Enterprise customers can configure backup retention up to 365 days.
When an account is deleted, its data is permanently purged after 90 days.
Audit logs for Enterprise accounts are retained for 1 year.
