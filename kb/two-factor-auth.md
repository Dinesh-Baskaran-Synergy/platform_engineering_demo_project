# Two-Factor Authentication

Two-factor authentication (2FA) can be enabled under Account Settings > Security.
AcmeCloud supports authenticator apps and hardware security keys for 2FA.
SMS-based 2FA is not supported.
When you enable 2FA you receive 10 single-use recovery codes.
If you lose your 2FA device and recovery codes, account recovery requires identity verification and takes up to 3 business days.
