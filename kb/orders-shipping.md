# AcmeEdge Hardware Orders and Shipping

AcmeCloud sells AcmeEdge appliances for on-premises edge computing.
Order IDs use the format ORD- followed by four digits, for example ORD-1234.
Standard shipping takes 3 to 5 business days within the US and EU.
You can check the status of an order by giving its order ID to the support assistant.
AcmeEdge appliances can be returned unused within 30 days of delivery.
