# Support Channels and Response Times

Free and Starter customers get email support with a 48-hour response time.
Pro customers get 24/7 chat support with a 4-hour response time.
Enterprise customers get a 1-hour response time for critical issues and a named account manager.
Support tickets can be created in the help center or by the support assistant.
Ticket priorities are low, normal, high and urgent.
