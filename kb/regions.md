# Regions and Data Residency

AcmeCloud operates three regions: us-east (Virginia), eu-west (Dublin) and ap-south (Mumbai).
Customer data stays in the region you select when creating a project.
Moving a project to another region requires a support ticket and may take up to 48 hours.
The eu-west region is recommended for customers with EU data residency requirements.
