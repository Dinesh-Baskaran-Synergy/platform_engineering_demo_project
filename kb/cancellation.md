# Cancelling or Downgrading

You can cancel your subscription at any time from the billing portal.
After cancellation, the plan stays active until the end of the current billing period.
Downgrading from Pro to Starter takes effect at the next billing cycle.
Before downgrading, your storage usage must fit within the Starter limit of 50 GB.
Cancelling a subscription does not delete your account; account deletion is a separate step in Account Settings.
